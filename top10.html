The Official Top 10 Spots

The elite operations holding it down on the Indy blocks.

#1

Chris Fried Chicken

Restaurant Address • 3350 N High School Rd, Indianapolis, IN 46224

The chicken is the absolute star of the show. It features a flawlessly crispy, golden exterior that locks in an incredibly juicy and flavorful interior. Every bite is seasoned to perfection, proving that they truly master their craft. Beyond the phenomenal food, the staff is exceptionally friendly, attentive, and genuinely proud of what they serve. The restaurant is clean, inviting, and perfect for a casual meal with family or a quick, satisfying bite.

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#2

Rinconcito Salvadoreño

Restaurant Address • 3360 W Morris St, Indianapolis, IN 46217

For an unbeatable taste of Central American comfort food, this incredibly fresh, family-owned gem serves up incredible handcrafted specialties made with traditional recipes and fresh ingredients. The absolute stars of the menu are their legendary pupusas—freshly made, packed with savory fillings, and served with tangy curtido cabbage slaw. With massive flavor, generous portions, and friendly service, it delivers an exceptionally authentic dining experience.

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#3

Everest Momo

Restaurant Address • 3816 N High School Rd, Indianapolis, IN 46254

Everest Momo is an absolute gem! The food is incredibly fresh, and their momos are perfectly juicy and packed with flavor. You can tell everything is made with care. Beyond the amazing food, the service is outstanding. The staff is genuinely welcoming, attentive, and makes you feel right at home from the moment you walk in. Great vibes, excellent service, and delicious food—highly recommended!

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#4

Taqueria Oaxaca

Restaurant Address • 5101 W 38th St, Indianapolis, IN 46254

Taqueria Oaxaca is fantastic! The food tastes like a homemade meal, and the tacos are packed with authentic flavor and fresh ingredients. Their salsas have the perfect kick. The service is just as wonderful as the food. Every staff member is friendly, quick, and genuinely happy to help you. It is a welcoming spot with incredible food and top-notch service—I will definitely be back!.

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#5

Taqueria El Barrigon

Restaurant Address • 4455 W Washington St, Indianapolis, IN 46241

Holding down an incredible 4.8-star reputation on the west side pavement, Taqueria El Barrigon is an absolute masterclass in authentic Mexican street flavors. The seasoned meats are the true headline act—the carne asada is grilled to absolute juicy perfection with crisp edges, and their al pastor features a beautiful, deep-chili marinade balance. Beyond their elite street tacos, their giant tortas and massive burritos are legendary among local drivers, packed heavy with fresh ingredients that never skimp on portion sizes. The entire setup is immaculately clean, fast, and driven by an exceptionally welcoming neighborhood staff proud of their craft. A certified must-visit destination on the Indy block.

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#6

Mirza's 504 Restaurant 2

Restaurant Address • 6320 W 38th St, Indianapolis, IN 46254

Mirza's 504 Restaurant II truly earns its 4.6-star rating by delivering an exceptional dining experience from start to finish. From the moment you walk into the elegant, pristine dining area, you are welcomed by incredibly warm and attentive staff who treat you like family. The menu is packed with authentic, flavorful Honduran dishes—like their perfectly seasoned Pollo con Tajadas and fresh Baleadas—all served in generous portions that give you a comforting, home-cooked taste. With fair prices, top-tier customer service, and an immaculate environment, it is a genuine hidden gem that is perfect for any family dinner!

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#7

The Dominican Flavor Food

Restaurant Address • 5108 W 38 St, Indianapolis, IN 46254

If you are looking for true, authentic Caribbean street food, The Dominican Flavor Food Truck is hands down the best spot in town. The food is packed with incredible seasoning, the portions are massive, and the service is exceptionally warm, helpful, and welcoming—making you feel right at home from the moment you order.

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#8

El Tablon Restaurant

Restaurant Address • 5834 E Washington St, Indianapolis, IN 46219

A hidden gem on the east side! El Tablón is bright, spotless, and incredibly welcoming. The food is fresh, authentic, and packed with flavor. We tried the savory empanadas and the sweet corn cachapas loaded with tender meat and cheese, and every single bite was delicious. The portions are generous, the owners are wonderful, and the service makes you feel right at home. If you want a true culinary trip to Latin America, this 5-star spot is an absolute must-visit!

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#9

DeliJey Food Truck Hondureño

Restaurant Address • 3585 commercial Dr, Indianapolis, IN, 46222

I ordered delivery from this amazing Honduran food truck, and it completely blew me away! The food arrived hot, fresh, and securely packaged, tasting just as delicious as if I were eating right at the window. The pollo con tajadas (fried chicken with crispy green banana slices) was absolute perfection—the chicken was super juicy on the inside with an incredible crunch, and the toppings tied everything together. I also got some cheesy, gooey pupusas and a fresh baleada that felt like pure comfort food. Huge portions, wonderful authentic flavors, and lightning-fast delivery. If you want a real taste of Honduras delivered straight to your door, do not hesitate to order from them!"

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
#10

Deisy's Latin Food Kitchen

Restaurant Address • 2915 English Ave, Indianapolis, IN, 46201

Absolutely incredible food and hospitality!If you are looking for authentic, flavorful Latin comfort food, Deisy’s Latin Food Kitchen is a must-visit. Every single plate feels like it was cooked with genuine love and tradition. The tacos and nachos are hands down some of the best you will ever have in town. The atmosphere is warm, casual, and incredibly welcoming. The staff makes you feel right at home from the moment you walk in. I cannot recommend this hidden gem enough!

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
