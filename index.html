The Indy Street Food History

The Late-Night Anchors (Pre-2010): Born on the pavement of West Washington, 38th Street, and Pendleton Pike. Cash-only setups built entirely on working-class word-of-mouth.

The Gourmet Spark (2010–2018): Indy ranked the 2nd best city in America for food truck friendliness by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation, spiking a massive mobile startup boom.

The Modern Explosion (2019–Present): A universal Indiana statewide permit passed, removing confusing county laws. With 100+ active trucks out there, the gap between elite masters and sloppy setups is massive.

#1
Chris Fried Chicken
Restaurant Address • 3350 N High School Rd, Indianpolis, IN 46224

The chicken is the absolute star of the show. It features a flawlessly crispy, golden exterior that locks in an incredibly juicy and flavorful interior. Every bite is seasoned to perfection, proving that they truly master their craft.Beyond the phenomenal food, the staff is exceptionally friendly, attentive, and genuinely proud of what they serve. #2
Rinconcito Salvadoreño
Restaurant Address • 3360 W Morris St, Indianapolis, IN 46217

For an unbeatable taste of Central American comfort food, Rinconcito Salvadoreño is a local must-visit. This welcoming, family-owned gem serves up incredible handcrafted specialties made with traditional recipes and fresh ingredients. The absolute stars of the menu are their legendary pupusas—freshly made, packed with savory fillings, and served with tangy curtido cabbage slaw. With massive flavor, generous portions, and friendly service, it delivers an exceptionally authentic dining experience.

Rating: ★★★★★
#3
Everest Momo
Restaurant Address • 3816 N High School RD, Indianapolis, IN 46254

Everest Momo is an absolute gem! The food is incredibly fresh, and their momos are perfectly juicy and packed with flavor. You can tell everything is made with care. Beyond the amazing food, the service is outstanding. The staff is genuinely welcoming, attentive, and makes you feel right at home from the moment you walk in. Great vibes, excellent service, and delicious food—highly recommended!.

Rating: ★★★★★
#4
Taqueria Oaxaca
Restaurant Address • 5101 W 38th St, Indianapolis, IN 46254

Taqueria Oaxaca is fantastic! The food tastes like a homemade meal, and the tacos are packed with authentic flavor and fresh ingredients. Their salsas have the perfect kick. The service is just as wonderful as the food. Every staff member is friendly, quick, and genuinely happy to help you. It is a welcoming spot with incredible food and top-notch service—I will definitely be back!.

Rating: ★★★★★
