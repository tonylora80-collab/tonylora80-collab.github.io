About Us

Welcome to No Filter Food Trucks & Restaurants!

We are dedicated to bringing you the ultimate guide to Indianapolis's neighborhood food blocks, helping locals and visitors discover the best local eats, hidden gems, and community street flavors across the city.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to connect hungry food lovers directly with local food truck operators and small restaurants. We believe in transparency, raw community reviews, and supporting local culinary entrepreneurs without any filters or corporate bias.

Contact Our Team

We love hearing from our community. If you run a local food truck or restaurant in the Indianapolis area and want to update your schedule, business text line, or list your location on our directory, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team.
