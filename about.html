About Us

Welcome to No Filter Food Trucks & Restaurants, the premier, unfiltered culinary resource mapping the authentic neighborhood blocks of Indianapolis. This media property is owned, published, and operated by CC Marketing Media Group LLC.

Our platform was born out of a simple reality: mainstream food reviews focus almost entirely on trendy, corporate downtown developments while completely ignoring the hidden culinary masterminds serving neighborhood streets. Curated by an experienced Google Maps Local Guide with over 750,000 verified views, we hit the pavement on 38th, Lafayette, Washington, and beyond to find real food managed by hard-working local operators.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to connect hungry food lovers directly with local food truck operators and independent brick-and-mortar neighborhood small businesses. We believe in absolute transparency, raw community-driven metrics, and supporting culinary entrepreneurs without any filters, corporate biases, or paid protection. If a kitchen drops fire, we praise it heavily. If a dish is dry, old, or overpriced, it lands immediately on our public Need to Improve List. We judge strictly on food execution, portion value, and service consistency—never the neighborhood.

Corporate Transparency & Contact

As a digital publication under CC Marketing Media Group LLC, we maintain a strict firewall against corporate influence. We pay for all of our food with cash, eat in our vehicles, and refuse corporate bribes or free promotional meals to ensure our data remains 100% authentic. If you run a local food truck or neighborhood restaurant in the Indianapolis area and need to update your operating schedule, submit a business text line, or list your active location on our directory database, please reach out directly to our operations team.
